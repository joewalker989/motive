# Wellbeing content: independent review still needed

Motive provides optional general wellbeing activities. This document prepares an external review; it does not certify clinical safety or effectiveness, and no professional has been represented as approving this content.

Review scope: the session wording and two-part optional breathing rhythms in `wellbeing-core.js`; the 14 daily pauses and movement wording in `growth-core.js`; the grounding → selected pause → quiet transitions in `calm-tools-core.js`; and the original stories in `rest-tools-core.js`.

The interface offers natural breathing, no breath holds, an immediate switch to grounding, stopping at any time, and guidance to stop counts if uncomfortable or dizzy. The one-tap entry uses a silent one-minute grounding session. Optional synthetic sound and bells are off unless selected. None of these activities diagnoses a condition or replaces care.

An independent suitably qualified reviewer should check the words, timing, accessibility, potentially uncomfortable attention to bodily sensations, culturally inclusive alternatives, and the advice for stopping or seeking help. Their review should identify the exact commit, proposed changes, qualifications, review date, and limitations. Store only their authorized public findings here; do not publish private contact or health information.

Reference for general gentle, comfortable, unforced breathing: [NHS breathing exercises for stress](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/breathing-exercises-for-stress/). This is a reference, not endorsement of Motive or its particular counts.
